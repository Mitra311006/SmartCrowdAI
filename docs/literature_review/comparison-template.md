# Literature comparison template

Use one row per paper. Keep reported facts separate from reviewer interpretation; cite the exact source in the DOI/official URL field. Write “not reported” or “not verified” rather than guessing. Label reviewer-suggested gaps “Project hypothesis.”

| Paper title | Authors | Year | Publication venue | DOI or official URL | Research problem | Model | Dataset | Metrics | Results | Limitations | Potential research gaps |

**Evidence discipline:** describe dataset, metric definitions, numerical results and limitations only when confirmed from accessible scholarly/publisher sources. Do not compare numbers across incompatible datasets or protocols. “Potential research gaps” records your own synthesis/hypotheses, not claims attributed to authors unless clearly stated as such.
